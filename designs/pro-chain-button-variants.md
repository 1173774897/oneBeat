Variant direction — LOOSE SINGLE LOOP:

One visible front-facing strand sweeps gently from the top edge at about 70% of the button's width to the bottom edge at about 88%, with a little slack making a shallow curved sag rather than a ruler-straight diagonal. It turns around the bottom edge, disappears behind the solid button, and a short arc reappears over the far-right top edge to complete the sense of a wrapped loop. The free end settles over the lower-right rim and descends just outside the button. Make the chain asymmetrical and relaxed, not an X, not two intersecting diagonals and not a decorative necklace drawn across the whole button.
Hanging tail: 4–5 small links nearly vertical, slight relaxed sideways drift, followed by the tiny ring and pendant. Rounded natural movement under gravity, no upward curl. Prioritize simple topology and convincing weight over embellishment.
